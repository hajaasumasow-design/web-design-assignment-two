What this site does not do

It does not take payments, hold rooms or sign contracts. Listings here are examples, and you should confirm every detail with the provider before paying anything.

Questions to ask any provider

What does the rent include?

Ask about water, electricity, internet and any service charges.

How much is the deposit, and when is it returned?

Get the amount, the conditions and the refund timeline in writing.

How long is the contract?

Check the start and end dates, and what happens if you leave early.

How far is it from campus, and how do students get there?

Walk the route if you can, and consider it after dark.
